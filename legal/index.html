The fine print

Leap Gate is a concept product. These pages describe how the demo behaves — plainly, without legalese.

Privacy Cookies Terms

Privacy

The Leap Gate demo has no server. Your profile, focus sessions, squads, presets and settings are saved in your own browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

  • Passwords typed on the log-in or sign-up screen are checked for length and then discarded — they are never stored.
  • "Continue with Google/Apple" in the demo does not contact those services; it simply creates a local sample profile.
  • Invite and download emails are simulated; no email is sent.
  • You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Your data.

Cookies

We don't set tracking or advertising cookies. The only thing stored is the local-storage record that keeps your demo data between visits. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Terms

The demo is provided as-is for evaluation. Focus statistics are motivational, not medical advice. Please be kind in squads, and don't use the app while driving — Pip can wait.
