LEAP GATE IS A FOCUS GAME

Most productivity apps nag you with lists. Leap Gate gives your attention somewhere to go. Every focused minute adds distance to Pip's flight; switch to a feed and Pip drifts down to land. It is gentle on purpose — the goal is a better habit, not a guilt trip.

PICK YOUR MODE

Sprint for 25 minutes, go deep for 90, or plan a custom flight. Each mode has its own soundtrack, break rhythm and landing animation, so switching gears feels like changing altitude.

WHO WE ARE —
A TEAM THAT GOT DISTRACTED

We are four people who kept opening a timer app and closing it thirty seconds later. Streak counters felt like homework and blockers felt like punishment, so we built something that rewards staying put instead. Pip started as a doodle on a sticky note.

OUR MISSION: MAKE FOCUS FEEL LIGHT

Attention is the most valuable thing you spend each day. Leap Gate makes protecting it feel less like willpower and more like play — and keeps every minute of your data on your own device.
