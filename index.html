LEAP IS A PRECISION MARKET

Most forecasting apps want a yes or a no. LEAP wants a figure. Every call in a pool gets scored on its distance from the close, how early it arrived and how long it held; your cut is that score multiplied by your stake. Nobody loses outright. Some calls just land further away.

PICK A POOL

Every pool is a single asset and a single close time. Scroll through what's running — tokens, indices and chain memes — see the pot, the entry count and the countdown, then drop your figure before it locks.

WHO WE ARE —
ON-CHAIN, IN THE OPEN

We kept calling the move and missing the magnitude, and every app we tried only cared about the move. So we made one that cares about the magnitude. LEAP runs on Nova Chain, publishes its scoring, and pays out USDX with ordinary transfers anyone can check.

OUR MISSION: REWARD PRECISION

A forecast should be a figure, not a guess at a direction. LEAP pays the players who are nearest, earliest and most steady — and puts the proof on a leaderboard for everyone to see.
